Delete your SpendWise account

This page explains how to delete your SpendWise account and the data linked to it.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open SpendWise and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account & sync, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with Google or Apple.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email surajsalunkedev@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my SpendWise account". We'll delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address, name and user ID).
  • All expenses and categories synced to the cloud.
  • When you delete in the app, the copy on that device too.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Copies on other devices that are signed out stay on those devices until you uninstall the app.
