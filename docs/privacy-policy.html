SpendWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

SpendWise is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. SpendWise is developed by Suraj Salunke ("we").

Data you enter

The expenses you record (amount, category, note, date, payment method) and the categories you create. This is stored on your device in the app's private storage.

Account and cloud sync (optional)

You can use SpendWise without an account. If you choose to sign in (with Google, Apple or email and password), we store:

  • your email address, name (if your sign-in provider shares it) and a user ID, in Firebase Authentication;
  • a copy of your expenses and categories, in Cloud Firestore, so they sync between your devices.

Firebase is a service of Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google. Only you can read your synced data; our security rules block access by anyone else. See Firebase's privacy information.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The app doesn't read your SMS, contacts, location or bank accounts.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent to do this.

Backups

On Android, your device may back up the app's expense database to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup), only where the backup can be end-to-end encrypted. Your sign-in session is never included.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, all your synced data and the copy on that device.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • If you never signed in, uninstalling the app deletes all your data.

Children

SpendWise is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
